## What the counts say

* **Stage 3 (candidate), 6 contacts - 5 of them serves - is the largest bucket,
  and the evidence is unambiguous: the detector SEES the ball (raw detections
  at conf 0.74-0.87, none suppressed) but the ball tracker is `unlocked` and
  says `unlocked_no_motion` on 19-25 of the 31 window frames.** The only lock
  in the window is a single `bootstrap_locked` frame, after which the coast
  dies (`coast_short_trajectory`) and the re-lock never happens: the toss
  apex is slower than the `lock_min_speed = 8 px/f` bar the bootstrap needs.
  No GT contact dies at raw detection (stage 1) or at track admission with
  detections present *and* the track held through the window (stage 2).
* **Stage 5 (actor/team), 5 contacts, 4 of them P8** - exactly the post-P7
  side switch the owner dictated (GT: near = B from P8). This is the known
  side-change limitation (STATUS open point 2 / plan task T14), not a
  detection problem, and it is orthogonal to the serve class.
* **Stage 4 (reach gate), 5 contacts** (P5 f2575, P7 f3782/f3950/f4002,
  f3747) - a real but smaller class.
* **Stage 6 (label), 4 contacts** - `overpass` read as `spike`/`set`/`dig`
  (3) and P7 f3852's set read as `serve`.
* **The serve class is the single biggest deficit overall:** 5 missed serves
  plus 4 false-positive `serve` actions (f1039, f2195, f2414, f3595) - the
  same toss class, mistimed rather than missing.

### T5 recommendation (ONE mechanism)

**Ball-tracker (re-)admission at the serve toss**, not time-based windows and
not the width-split: extend the UNLOCKED bootstrap so a candidate with
sustained consecutive sightings can lock on motion evidence weaker than
`lock_min_speed = 8 px/f` when it is *ascending* (a toss), instead of
requiring a near-consecutive pair that a slow apex never satisfies. It
addresses 5 GT deaths + the 4 serve FPs (9 of 28 contacts touched) and nothing
else in the table does. It is the "candidate recovery" family of the plan's
option list, but the evidence places the loss one layer EARLIER: the
classifier never gets a trajectory because the tracker never held the ball.

Validation for that mechanism (next session): the 7 entreno action logs must
stay byte-identical-or-better (serves exist on e1-e7), plus the dev clip, and
the serve-FP count must not grow.
